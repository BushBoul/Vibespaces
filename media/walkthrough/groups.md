# Window groups

Some projects go together: a website and its server, or your dashboard next to the code behind it. A **group**
opens them side by side, each in its own spot on the screen, and puts them all back with one shortcut.

### Make one

1. In the Vibespaces side bar, open **Groups** and click **+**.
2. Name it, for example *Trading*.
3. Tick the spaces that belong together.
4. For each one, choose where it goes: its own spot, or a spot just for this group (left column, right half…).

### Use it

- **▶ (Open Group):** opens every space in the group in its own window, each in its spot. One that's already open
  just moves into place.
- **⌂ or Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Home:** puts the whole group back. Each window does what its own Ctrl+Alt+Home does (its
  panels, side bars and tab tidying, as set in the space), then moves to its spot and comes to the front. In a space
  that's in more than one group, it's the group you opened last. With no groups at all, it puts every open space
  back.

**From any app:** Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Home works even when VS Code isn't in front. It puts back the group of the space you
used last and brings that space's window to the front, ready to type in. Turn it off or change the key under
**Settings** in the Vibespaces side bar.

**A key for each group:** right-click a group and choose **Group Shortcut…**, then type a key such as
`ctrl+alt+shift+1`. That key puts just that group back and brings it to the front, from any app. Give your other
groups 2, 3 and so on. A space can be in several groups: in each, it goes to that group's spot.

### Change it

Right-click a group to choose its spaces, rename it or delete it. Right-click a space in a group to change where it
goes in that group, or to take it out. Deleting a group never touches its spaces.
