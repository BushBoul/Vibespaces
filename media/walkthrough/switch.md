# Switch between spaces

There are three ways, use whichever is nearest:

- **The Vibespaces list:** click the Vibespaces icon (four small squares) in the bar on the far left, then click a
  space. The space this window has open is marked *this window*.
- **The bottom bar:** click the space's name at the bottom left of any window, and pick another space.
- **The keyboard:** press **Ctrl+Shift+P**, type *Open a Space*, press Enter.

Each space opens in its own window. If it's already open, VS Code takes you to that window instead of opening a
second one.

Closing a space's window doesn't lose anything: open it again and it comes back the way you left it.
