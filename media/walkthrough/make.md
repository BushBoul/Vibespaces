# Make your first space

### If your project is already open in this window

1. Click **Make This Window a Space** on the left (or in the Vibespaces list).
2. Type a name, for example *My Website*. It's shown in the title bar and on the taskbar.
3. Pick a colour.

The window closes and opens again as the space, in its colour. That's it.

### If it isn't open

1. Click **New Space…**.
2. Find your project's folder and click **Use for this space**.
3. Name it and pick a colour.

The space opens in a new window.

### Tips

- A space can hold more than one folder (say, a website and its server). Add more in the space's editor.
- Make one space per project. Opening a space you already have open just takes you to its window.
